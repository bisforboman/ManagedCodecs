# AVI audio (2026-11-08)

Issue #415's last open item for MP3 ("AVI audio", docs/decisions/2026-11-08-mp3-decoder.md, step 5). A plan, decided on by
the owner (below); nothing here is built yet.

## Why

`AviDemuxer.Audio` is always null (README: "AVI (video)"), so an AVI plays silent and `VideoProbe` leaves its audio
out of the duration (docs/conformance.md: avi-dropped-frame's MP3 outlasts its video). The AVIs this library can play
are MJPEG (cameras, capture cards) and H.264; their audio is MP3, PCM or, from cameras, IMA ADPCM. MP3 is the only one
of them the library decodes.

## How AVI stores audio, and what FFmpeg makes of it

A stream's 'strh' gives dwScale, dwRate and dwSampleSize, its 'strf' a WAVEFORMATEX (wFormatTag, channels, rate,
nBlockAlign; MPEGLAYER3WAVEFORMAT for MP3). Chunks `NNwb` in 'movi' hold the data, interleaved with the video's.

- **MP3 as FFmpeg's muxer writes it** (avi-dropped-frame): dwScale 1152, dwRate the sample rate, one frame a chunk.
  ffprobe: time base 1152/44100, a packet a chunk, pts 0, 1, 2, ...
- **MP3 as VirtualDub and older muxers write it** ("CBR"): dwScale 1, dwRate the bytes a second, dwSampleSize 1, chunks
  of any length, cut anywhere in a frame. FFmpeg's demuxer runs its MPEG audio parser over them (need_parsing) and gives
  a packet a frame, timed from the bytes before it.
- **PCM** (wFormatTag 1): dwSampleSize nBlockAlign; ffprobe gives a packet of 1024 samples, pts in samples (time
  base 1/rate), on FFmpeg's own PCM AVI (how it splits other chunk sizes: to be checked).
- **IMA ADPCM** (0x11), AC-3 (0x2000), AAC (0xFF): not yet checked against FFmpeg.

## Proposal

1. **`AviDemuxer.Audio`: the first audio stream**, its packets in `ReadPackets` (and `ReadPacketsAsync`,
   `ReadPacketInfo`) in file order with the video's, timed as ffprobe's packets: each checked against
   `ffprobe -show_packets` as the other demuxers are (packets by pts and MD5).
2. **MP3 in both layouts.** One frame a chunk as it comes; the CBR layout re-split into whole frames (the frame-header
   code `Mp3Demuxer` already has, `MpegAudioHeader`), each timed by the samples before it, as FFmpeg's parser does.
   `Mp3Decoder` takes whole frames only, so without this the most common AVI MP3 would not decode.
3. **Codec names:** `CodecIds.Mp3` (wFormatTag 0x55; 0x50, MPEG Layer I and II, only if FFmpeg reads Layer III
   frames under it too: to be checked), `CodecIds.Aac` (0xFF), others by their format tag (`"0x0001"` for PCM,
   `"0x0011"` for IMA ADPCM), as Matroska keeps a Codec ID it doesn't decode. `MediaPlayer` plays the MP3 and AAC ones; `SkippedAudioCodec` names the rest.
4. **Seek** (MJPEG only, as now): the audio starts at its last packet at or before the time asked for, as every
   demuxer's streams do (`SeekTests`); the time can be the audio stream's.
5. **`VideoProbe`'s duration** counts the audio, as ffprobe's does.
6. **Release:** no new public members (`Audio` exists), but `ReadPackets` now gives audio packets in AVI files and
   `VideoProbe`'s duration can change: a "Changed" line, in the next minor version.

## Samples

FFmpeg's muxer writes one-frame-a-chunk MP3 (avi-dropped-frame; a stereo one beside H.264, `avi-h264-mp3`) and PCM
(`avi-mjpeg-pcm`). No encoder at hand writes the CBR layout: `tools/avi-mp3-cbr.py` rewrites an FFmpeg AVI's audio into
it (chunks merged and cut at arbitrary bytes, 'strh' and 'strf' rewritten), and the reference is ffprobe's packets of
the rewritten file, so that FFmpeg's parser, not the script, says what the packets are.

## Decided

Asked with the multiple-choice prompt; the owner took each proposal (2026-11-08).

1. **Scope. Decided:** every AVI audio stream exposed; MP3 decoded and played in both layouts; PCM, IMA ADPCM and the
   rest named and skipped (no PCM decoder for now).
2. **Names. Decided:** the format tag in hex (`"0x0001"`), no new `CodecIds` constants.
3. **Release. Decided:** a "Changed" line, in the next minor version.
4. **CBR MP3's times. Decided** (asked while building): each frame at its sample position (time base 1 / the sample
   rate). FFmpeg's packets there are its parser's interpolation in a byte's time base: on avi-mjpeg-mp3-cbr up to 10
   ticks (1.25 ms) behind each frame's byte offset, exact for 11 of 78 frames. The frames' bytes are compared with
   FFmpeg's exactly, their times within 1.5 ms.

## Done (2026-11-08)

Step 2 as planned, with the answers above. What building it found:

- FFmpeg's AVI muxer drops a video frame (an empty chunk, the second) while interleaving audio, as in
  avi-dropped-frame: the new samples have it too.
- FFmpeg writes the chunk id ('01wb') in the header's OpenDML 'indx' too: search 'movi' for the chunks.
- PCM's packets on FFmpeg's own file are its chunks (2048 bytes of 22.05 kHz mono s16), timed in samples. 0x50 was
  not checked (no decoder takes it yet).
- Later (2026-11-09): how FFmpeg splits other chunk sizes, checked with ffprobe on FFmpeg AVIs whose PCM chunks were
  merged: pieces of 1024 blocks below 32 bytes a block, one block from 32 up (IMA and MS ADPCM), whole chunks at 1
  byte, each timed by all the bytes before it. The demuxer cuts them so (docs/conformance.md, AVI audio).
- H.264 in AVI (Annex B, no avcC) isn't decoded, audio or not: the H.264 sample planned became MJPEG. (Decoded since:
  docs/decisions/2026-11-09-h264-in-avi.md.)
