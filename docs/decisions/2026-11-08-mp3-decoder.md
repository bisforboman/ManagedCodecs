# An MP3 decoder (2026-11-08)

Issue #415, step 3. A plan, decided on by the owner (below); nothing here is built yet. Steps 1 and 2 of the issue
(`MediaPlayer.SkippedAudioCodec`, `CodecIds.Mp3`, the README) are in the same pull request, #418.

## Why

A video with MP3 audio plays silent. MP3 in video files is mostly older material: Tears of Steel (one of the README's
two test clips for the engines), MOV and AVI from the 2000s, many AVI rips. AAC covers nearly everything recorded or
encoded today. So it is worth having, not urgent. The patents expired in 2017.

## What

- **A package of its own, `ManagedCodecs.Mp3`**, like each codec: `Mp3Decoder : IAudioDecoder`.
  - Constructors: `Mp3Decoder(AudioStreamInfo stream)` and `Mp3Decoder()`. MP3 has no codec private data: every frame
    header carries the rate, the channels and the layer.
  - Members: `Decode(ReadOnlySpan<byte>)`, `Decode(Packet)` (SkipSamples, SkipEndSamples and Discard, as AAC's),
    `SampleRate` and `Channels` (from the first frame decoded, 0 before one).
  - No `Threads`. A frame is 1152 samples of cheap work, and AAC needed threads only for SBR.
  - Every member is an API decision (PublicAPI files, package validation, CHANGELOG), so it ships in a new minor
    version. The meta-package, `ManagedCodecs.Unity` (its sources) and the WebAssembly build take it like the others.
- **Scope of the first version:** Layer III, all three of its sample rate families: MPEG-1 (32, 44.1, 48 kHz), MPEG-2
  LSF (16, 22.05, 24) and MPEG-2.5 (8, 11.025, 12). That covers mono, stereo, joint stereo (mid/side and intensity,
  both MPEG-1's and LSF's intensity rules), long, short and mixed blocks, and the bit reservoir. Free-format bitrates
  are included only if a sample can be made (see Samples).
- **Spec:** ISO/IEC 11172-3 (MPEG-1 audio) and ISO/IEC 13818-3 (its LSF extension); MPEG-2.5 is Fraunhofer's extension
  of the latter (no standard: its tables follow from 13818-3 halved). It is implemented from the spec, never by porting
  FFmpeg's (LGPL) or libmad's (GPL). minimp3 is CC0, so its tables may be cross-checked against ours, as Apache-2.0
  decoders were for MJPEG (README's License section would list it).
- **Playback:** `MediaPlayer` plays MP3 as it plays AAC. `SkippedAudioCodec` (#418) then names only what is left
  (Opus, Vorbis, AC-3, PCM).

## The decoder's stages and their references

Correct first, then fast, as the rest of the library:

1. **Header and side info**, with the CRC checked when present (a mismatch is damage: InvalidDataException, or the
   concealment below).
2. **The bit reservoir.** A frame's main data starts up to 511 bytes back, in earlier frames' packets, so the decoder
   keeps them. After a seek, or on the first packet of a stream cut out of a longer one, the data isn't there. What
   FFmpeg outputs for such a granule is the oracle (to be checked: silence, or the frame dropped). That is behaviour
   FFmpeg chooses rather than the spec, so it gets a mutation entry.
3. **Huffman decoding** (the 32 big-value tables and the two count1 tables) and **requantisation**: x^(4/3) through
   the AAC decoder's `FourThirdsPower`, not `Math.Pow`, which differs in its last bit on the WebAssembly runtime (AAC's
   CLAUDE.md), and 2^(x/4) through `QuarterPowerOfTwo`.
4. **Stereo processing** (mid/side, intensity), **reordering** of short blocks, **alias reduction**.
5. **IMDCT** (36 and 12 points, the four window shapes, overlap-add) and **frequency inversion**.
6. **The polyphase synthesis filterbank** (32 subbands).

Every fast path keeps the definition as its reference, compared on random input: the IMDCT by its sum, and the
synthesis by the matrixing and windowing of 11172-3 Annex A (a fast version goes through a 32-point DCT, possibly
vectorised later). Tables are computed in arithmetic, or written out as the spec gives them, where `Math.Cos` and
`Math.Sin` could differ between runtimes (AAC's `QmfTrig` lesson).

## Conformance

- **Against FFmpeg** (`mp3float`, its default MP3 decoder, which outputs float as ours does): every sample by value,
  as AAC's are (name.f32). The tolerance is set from what float rounding in the synthesis allows, to be measured: AAC LC
  reaches 1e-6.
- **Against the spec's own criterion** (11172-4): an RMS difference from the reference below 2^-15 / sqrt(12) for a
  "full accuracy" decoder. This applies if the 11172-4 compliance bitstreams can be obtained under a licence that
  allows committing them. If not, it is checked against FFmpeg's double-precision output, if one is reachable.
- **Real films:** Tears of Steel's audio joins `realworld.yml` (its video is already compared).

## Timing and priming

- MP4: the edit list carries the encoder's delay. LAME's delay plus the decoder's 529 samples is 1105, which FFmpeg's
  muxer writes and the demuxer already gives as SkipSamples (h264-mp3, #418). Nothing new is needed.
- Matroska: FFmpeg's muxer writes no CodecDelay for MP3, so the priming plays, as FFmpeg plays it.
- Raw `.mp3` files (ID3v2, the Xing/LAME tag's delay and padding for gapless playback) need a demuxer of their own, as
  ADTS has. This is a later step, below.
- AVI: `AviDemuxer.Audio` is null by design (README: "AVI (video)"), and AVI's MP3 chunks are not cut at frame
  boundaries. Exposing AVI audio is a demuxer decision of its own, below.

## Samples

FFmpeg here has two MP3 encoders: libmp3lame and libshine (fixed point, a different encoder's choices). Each sample is
checked to exercise its tool through the decoder's internal statistics, as AAC's are, and the src/ManagedCodecs.Mp3
CLAUDE.md records which encoder writes what:

- LAME at each sample rate family, CBR and VBR (the reservoir's reach varies), mono, stereo, joint stereo, and a
  transient-heavy signal for short blocks.
- shine for a second encoder.
- Mixed blocks, intensity stereo and free format: if neither encoder writes them, a script rewrites a stream (as
  `tools/make-aac-pulse.py` writes AAC's pulse data), or unit tests cover them and the docs say so.

## Order (each its own pull request)

1. **This plan** (#418, with steps 1 and 2 of the issue).
2. **`ManagedCodecs.Mp3`**: Layer III decoded from MP4 and Matroska, against FFmpeg. Scalar first.
3. **Playback** plays MP3; mcplay, the Godot node and the Unity sample with it; Tears of Steel with sound.
4. **Speed**, measured first: a fast synthesis and IMDCT with their references. Likely small next to video decoding.
   The filterbanks done (2026-11-08): 342 to 84 µs a frame of Tears of Steel's MP3, FFmpeg 54, with the definitions'
   bits (docs/conformance.md, MP3, Speed); Huffman decoding and requantization, now the largest part, are left.
5. **Later, each its own decision:** a raw `.mp3` demuxer, AVI audio, and Layers I and II (close to Layer III's
   front end, no Huffman or IMDCT; mostly broadcast and Video CD material).

## Decided (the owner, 2026-11-08)

1. **The package and its API** as above: `ManagedCodecs.Mp3`, `Mp3Decoder` in the AAC decoder's shape.
2. **Layer III only at first.** Layers I and II are a later decision.
3. **`CodecIds.Mp3` stays** (#418), ahead of the decoder. This amends the Matroska decision (2026-11-03): "a constant
   names a codec the library handles" now includes a codec this plan commits to handling, so a video playing silent
   can say "MP3" rather than a sample entry's name (`mp4a`).
4. **A raw `.mp3` demuxer and AVI audio come later**, each its own decision. The decoder starts with MP4 and Matroska.

## Size

Less than AAC (3550 lines with SBR and PS), since there is no SBR or PS and the bitstream is simpler. The same stages
remain: Huffman, dequantisation, stereo, IMDCT and filterbank. An estimate: 1500 to 2000 lines with the tests, and two
or three pull requests to get from step 2 to step 3.
