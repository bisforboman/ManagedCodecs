# An IMA ADPCM decoder for AVI audio (2026-11-10)

After PCM (docs/decisions/2026-11-09-pcm-decoder.md), the other audio cameras write beside MJPEG in AVI is IMA ADPCM
(format tag 0x11): `AviDemuxer` names it `"0x0011"` and cuts its packets as FFmpeg does (a block each, #446), but
nothing decodes it, so such files play silent. Decided and built in one PR.

## What AVI gives

- FFmpeg's muxer writes a block a chunk (1024 bytes by default, `-block_size` 32 to 8192), dwScale wSamplesPerBlock
  and dwRate the sample rate: packets a block each, timed in blocks, as ffprobe gives them (and `AviDemuxer` already
  does). Merged chunks are cut a block a packet (docs/conformance.md, AVI audio).
- The WAVEFORMATEX's 2 extra bytes, `CodecPrivate`, are wSamplesPerBlock; wBitsPerSample is 4 from FFmpeg's encoder,
  the only one at hand. FFmpeg also decodes 2, 3 and 5 bits (rare).

## What FFmpeg makes of it

Its `adpcm_ima_wav` decoder gives 16-bit samples, which `-f f32le` turns into exactly x / 32768. A block of B bytes
gives 1 + 8 * floor((B - 4 * channels) / (4 * channels)) samples a channel: the header's (the first sample, then the
step index and a reserved byte, 4 bytes a channel), then 8 for each group of 4 bytes, the channels' groups in turn, low
nibble first. A block cut short gives its whole groups; one shorter than its headers is an error.

The difference added for a nibble differs from IMA's recommended practice. The practice sums step, step / 2, step / 4
and step / 8 by shifts, each rounded down on its own. FFmpeg computes (2 * magnitude + 1) * step / 8, the same
(magnitude + 1/2) * step / 4 rounded down once, up to 2 higher at each step (checked on hand-built blocks:
`ImaAdpcmDecoderTests`). Every sample here is checked against FFmpeg exactly, so the decoder rounds once too; a
mutation entry puts the practice's shifts back and needs a test to fail.

## Decided (the owner, 2026-11-10)

1. **Name:** a new constant `CodecIds.AdpcmImaWav` (`"ADPCM_IMA_WAV"`, as FFmpeg's `adpcm_ima_wav`), given by
   `AviDemuxer` at 4 bits only, as PCM is named by its depth; 2, 3 and 5 bits stay `"0x0011"` and skipped.
2. **Scope:** IMA only. Microsoft ADPCM (0x02), another algorithm with its own tables, is a later decision.
3. **Where:** `ImaAdpcmDecoder : IAudioDecoder` in ManagedCodecs.Core beside `PcmDecoder`: about 200 lines and two
   small tables. It cuts a packet of several blocks by wSamplesPerBlock (a packet is one block without it), and
   honours SkipSamples, SkipEndSamples and Discard as the other decoders do. `MediaPlayer` plays it, `mcdec` decodes
   it (so the AOT, Unity and WebAssembly checks compare it).
4. **One PR** for this record and the decoder.

## Checks

- `avi-mjpeg-ima`: stereo at 22.05 kHz in 512-byte blocks, its decoded audio exactly FFmpeg's (name.ima.f32).
- Unit tests: blocks built by hand, decoded as FFmpeg decodes the same blocks in a WAV file (the rounding, a packet
  of several blocks, a block cut short, the channels' turns), malformed blocks, and the 4-bit rule.
- A CHANGELOG "Added" line, in the next minor version (new public API).
