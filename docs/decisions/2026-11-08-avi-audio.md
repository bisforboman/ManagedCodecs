# AVI audio (2026-11-08)

Issue #415, what the MP3 plan (2026-11-08-mp3-decoder.md) left to a decision of its own. A plan, decided on by the
owner (Decided, at the end); nothing here is built yet.

## Why

`AviDemuxer.Audio` is null by design: an AVI plays and probes as video only. So the MP3 in older AVIs (2000s rips,
VirtualDub captures, `avi-dropped-frame`) plays silent although `Mp3Decoder` decodes it, `VideoProbe` gives such a
file no audio facts, and the camera AVIs `realworld.yml` decodes (8-bit and 16-bit PCM at 11 kHz) have their sound
skipped. MP4, Matroska and raw `.mp3` already give their audio.

## What AVI stores, and what FFmpeg makes of it

Checked on files from FFmpeg's AVI muxer and on rewritten ones (2026-11-08, FFmpeg 6.1):

| Audio | strh dwScale / dwRate, dwSampleSize | strf (WAVEFORMATEX) | FFmpeg's packets and time base |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| MP3, as FFmpeg writes it (VBR style) | 1152 / (rate x 36), here 32 / 1225; 0 | 0x55, nBlockAlign 1152 | one frame a chunk; time base dwScale / dwRate, pts the chunk's index |
| MP3, CBR byte stream (older muxers) | 1 / nAvgBytesPerSec; 1 | 0x55, nBlockAlign 1 | chunks cut anywhere: FFmpeg's parser re-splits them into whole frames; time base 1 / nAvgBytesPerSec, pts about the frame's byte offset |
| PCM (0x1) | 1 / rate; block size | nBlockAlign = channels x bytes | one chunk a packet; time base 1 / rate, pts the samples before it |

FFmpeg trims no priming from AVI MP3 (there is nowhere to say it): the encoder's and decoder's delay play, as in
Matroska. The decoded audio is the same either way the MP3 is chunked (184,320 bytes of f32 for the 1 s sample, both
styles).

## Proposed

1. **`AviDemuxer.Audio`**: the first `auds` stream, as the MP4 and Matroska demuxers take theirs: `StreamIndex` its
   place among the streams, `SampleRate` and `Channels` from WAVEFORMATEX, `TimeBase` as FFmpeg's (table), the codec
   from wFormatTag: 0x55 `CodecIds.Mp3`; 0xFF and 0x1600 (ADTS) `CodecIds.Aac` with the AudioSpecificConfig after
   WAVEFORMATEX (if a sample can be made); others by their tag, `"0x2000"` style (AC-3 here), so
   `MediaPlayer.SkippedAudioCodec` can name them.
2. **Packets** in file order, video and audio interleaved as the 'movi' list has them (packet `Sequence` counting
   both, as the other demuxers do). Frame-a-chunk MP3 and PCM: a chunk, a packet. A CBR byte stream (dwSampleSize
   not 0, nBlockAlign 1): split into whole MP3 frames with `Mp3Demuxer`'s frame sync (internal, shared), a frame cut
   by a chunk boundary carried to the next chunk, each frame a packet at its byte offset, so its timestamps are
   FFmpeg's to the rounding the conformance tests allow (to be measured: FFmpeg's were within a byte of the offsets).
3. **PCM**: two choices, **(A) recommended**: expose it with a new `CodecIds.Pcm` constant (and the WAVEFORMATEX
   fields a decoder needs: bits per sample, in `AudioStreamInfo.CodecPrivate` as the raw WAVEFORMATEX), not decoded
   yet; **(B)**: also a `PcmDecoder` (8-bit unsigned, 16 and 24-bit signed, 32-bit float to float samples), a small
   new public type, so `MediaPlayer` plays the camera AVIs' sound and `realworld.py` compares it. Both are new public
   API (a new minor version).
4. **Seeking**: `Seek` stays MJPEG only (video keyframes); the audio after it starts at the first audio chunk after
   the video frame sought to, as FFmpeg's does without an index. The first MP3 frames then miss their reservoir and
   decode silent in part, as after any seek (`Mp3Decoder`).
5. **Not now**: `idx1`/`indx` (still not read; audio needs no keyframes), OpenDML files over 1 GB, a second audio
   stream, MPEG Layer II (0x50) beyond naming it.

## Samples and checks

- FFmpeg's muxer: MJPEG with MP3 (`avi-dropped-frame` already, and a VBR one), with PCM s16 and u8; their packets
  against `ffprobe -show_packets` (a `.packets.csv` per sample, as the other containers' tests do), MP3 decoded
  against FFmpeg's `mp3float` within 2^-17 (`Mp3ConformanceTests`, which takes every sample with a `.mp3.trim.f32`).
- A CBR byte stream with chunks cut mid-frame and no 'idx1', which FFmpeg's muxer doesn't write: a rewriting script
  (`tools/avi-rewrite.py`, from FFmpeg's file), as `tools/mkv-rewrite.py` is for Matroska.
- `VideoProbe` gives an AVI's audio facts (README's "AVI's audio is not read" goes); `MediaPlayer` plays AVI MP3
  (MediaPlayerTests); `realworld.yml`'s camera AVIs compare their PCM if (B).

## Order (each its own pull request)

1. `AviDemuxer.Audio` and its packets, MP3 and PCM exposed (and the codec constant of 3), the samples, the rewriting
   script; `VideoProbe` and `MediaPlayer` take it up (MP3 plays).
2. If (B): `PcmDecoder`, and `MediaPlayer` playing PCM.

## Size

The demuxer part is a few hundred lines with its tests, mostly the CBR frame splitting and the interleaved walk;
`PcmDecoder` well under a hundred.

## Decided (the owner, 2026-11-08)

1. **PCM: (A).** Exposed with a new `CodecIds.Pcm` and its WAVEFORMATEX as `CodecPrivate`, not decoded: `MediaPlayer`
   names it as skipped audio. A `PcmDecoder` is a later decision.
2. **Both MP3 styles in the first pull request**: frame-a-chunk files as they are, CBR byte streams split into whole
   frames with `Mp3Demuxer`'s frame sync, and a rewriting script for a sample of the second kind.
3. The rest as proposed: the first `auds` stream, packets in file order, `Seek` MJPEG only, no index, one pull request
   (step 2 of the order falls away with (A)).
