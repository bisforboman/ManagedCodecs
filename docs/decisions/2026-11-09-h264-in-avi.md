# H.264 in AVI (2026-11-09)

`AviDemuxer` named H.264 (FourCCs H264, X264, AVC1) but nothing decoded it: `H264Decoder` started from an avcC, which
FFmpeg's AVIs don't have, and `VideoProbe` threw on them (README's Open listed it after AVI audio, #446).

## What AVI holds, and what FFmpeg makes of it

- **FFmpeg's encoder** (libx264 into AVI): Annex B, NAL units after start codes (00 00 01, or 00 00 00 01 before the
  parameter sets and a picture's first slice), the SPS and PPS in the first packet; no avcC. A packet a picture.
- **Remuxed from MP4** (`ffmpeg -c copy`): the packets stay length-prefixed, and the avcC goes after the
  BITMAPINFOHEADER's 40 bytes, which FFmpeg's demuxer reads as extradata. Field pictures stay a packet a field.
- **Times:** AVI stores none but the chunks' order: ffprobe gives every H.264 packet dts = its decoding position and
  pts N/A. FFmpeg's decoder gives each frame the dts of the packet that brought it out, so with B pictures the video
  starts late by the reorder delay (2 frames, 80 ms, with libx264's two B frames); a frame its flush brings out gets
  none (ffprobe N/A; framemd5 fills in the time before plus one tick).

## Decided

Asked with the multiple-choice prompt (2026-11-09):

1. **Annex B in the decoder.** `H264Decoder` takes a stream without an avcC (empty codec private data) as Annex B:
   packets split at start codes, the zero bytes before one belonging to no NAL unit, the parameter sets in band. The
   packets stay FFmpeg's bytes, and a raw .h264 file could use it later. (Not: the demuxer rewriting packets to
   length-prefixed and building an avcC, which would make them differ from FFmpeg's.)
2. **Frame times in display order from 0.** Where the container stores no presentation times (AVI, and Annex B with
   no stream to say), the i-th frame shown takes the i-th smallest of the pictures' times (a second field's packet adds
   none): the first frame at the first packet's time, no shift. It differs from FFmpeg's by the reorder delay, the
   same for every frame, so H264ConformanceTests compares times from the first frame's, with ffprobe's frame times
   (name.frametimes: none for the frames FFmpeg's flush brings out, where framemd5 fills one in). (Not FFmpeg's times, which would tie the times to FFmpeg's reorder
   delay and start the video late; not refusing B pictures.)

## Built

- `VideoStreamInfo.DecodeOrderTimes` (internal: no public API) says the packets' times are decoding order; `AviDemuxer`
  sets it, and reads the BITMAPINFOHEADER's bytes after its 40 as `CodecPrivate` (an avcC where there is one).
- `H264Decoder.FrameSize` (for `VideoProbe`) gives null without an avcC: the probe takes the AVI header's size.
- Samples: `avi-h264-mp3` (libx264 with two B frames, 160x120, which crops the last macroblock row, beside stereo MP3:
  Annex B) and `avi-h264-paff` (JM's field pictures remuxed: length-prefixed with an avcC, a packet a field). Every H.264
  test runs on both: frames by MD5 against FFmpeg's, with threads, borrowed frames and asynchronously.
- Still not: `Seek` in H.264 AVI (needs the keyframes from 'idx1', not read), and so `MediaPlayer` seeking there.
